Chat Limits

  • All strings are sanitized to get rid of any malicious HTML/JS/CSS. Don't even try it, Only the contributors and/or no contributors can do itself.
  • There is a 2500 character limit in public rooms and a 5000 limit in private rooms.
    • Yes, the Navy Seals copypasta fits.
  • There is a 25 character limit on names.
    • Do you know that your name fits? Yeah, I know that!
  • A max of 8 people are allowed in a public lobby. After that, a new public room will be created.
    • Yeah, I know the creator changed the limit down from 15. With the limited screen space and processing power on mobile, this was part of the reason things were running so long enough.
  • A max of 30 people are allowed in private lobbies.

General Behavior

  • Don't spam. Repeated spam may result in a ban.
  • Don't harass other users.
  • Don't try to crash or exploit the server.

Remember that people can play YouTube videos whenever they want. Keep your volume down, headphone users.

Moderation

Chat rooms are for the most part unmoderated. However, if things get spambotty or break the rules above, the banhammer will come out.
